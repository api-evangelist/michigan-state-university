MSU had its most successful fundraising year in history. Between July 1, 2025, and June 30, 2026, 63,616 donors contributed more than $736 million to Michigan State University, reflecting the collective impact of Spartans and friends across all the globe and representing a 94% increase in giving from last year’s $380 million total fundraised.
